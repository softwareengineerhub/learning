  1) If you want to automatically send\receive Objects you need to add key/value serializer\deserializer to your configs
  For producer and consumer

  #spring.kafka.producer.key-serializer=org.springframework.kafka.support.serializer.JsonSerializer
  #spring.kafka.producer.value-serializer=org.springframework.kafka.support.serializer.JsonSerializer

  #spring.kafka.consumer.value-deserializer=org.springframework.kafka.support.serializer.JsonDeserializer
  #spring.kafka.consumer.key-deserializer=org.apache.kafka.common.serialization.StringDeserializer

  2)
  # Trust packages (adjust!)
  spring.kafka.consumer.properties.spring.json.trusted.packages=com.app.model
